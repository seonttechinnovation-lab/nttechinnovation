YouTube Subscriber Checklist for Indian Creators

Most small channels in India don't have a content problem. They have a "nobody knows why to subscribe" problem. People watch one video, like it and leave. This checklist fixes that, one habit at a time.

Every video

  • Say who the channel is for in the first 30 seconds. One line is enough.
  • Ask for the subscribe after you deliver value, not before.
  • End screen: point to one next video, not five.
  • Pin a comment that asks a real question. Replies keep people around.

Every week

  • Check which video brought the most new subscribers in YouTube Studio. Make one more like it.
  • Reply to every comment within a day.
  • Post one Short that teases a long video on the same topic.

Every month

  • Update the channel banner and trailer to match what the channel actually posts now.
  • Group videos into playlists by topic so binge-watching is easy.
