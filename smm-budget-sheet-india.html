Social Media Marketing in India: A Simple Budget Sheet for Small Businesses

Ask ten business owners in India what they spend on social media and nine will say not sure. That's the problem. Money goes out in small bits and nobody adds it up. This simple sheet fixes that.

Step 1: Pick a monthly number

Choose an amount you can keep paying for six months. A steady small budget beats one big month followed by silence.

Step 2: Split it four ways

  • Content, about 40%. Photos, Reels, design and captions. This is what people actually see.
  • Ads, about 35%. Boosted posts and lead ads aimed at your city or pin codes.
  • Tools and services, about 15%. Scheduling tools, a design app or a small panel order for a new page.
  • Testing, about 10%. Try one new idea each month, like a creator collab.

Step 3: Track three results

Enquiries received, orders closed and cost per enquiry. Write them down on the last day of the month. If cost per enquiry falls, you're on the right track.

Step 4: Move money toward what works

If ads bring cheap enquiries, shift 5% from testing to ads. If a Reel style keeps winning, spend more on content. Review once a month and not every day.

A note on agencies

If you hire help, ask for the same split in writing. You should know how much goes to ad spend and how much is the fee.
