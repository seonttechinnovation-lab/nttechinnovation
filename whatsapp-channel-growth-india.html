WhatsApp Channel Checklist for Indian Small Businesses

By MD Fozla Rabbi, SEO Specialist

WhatsApp is the one app almost every customer in India opens daily. A WhatsApp Channel lets you send updates to all of them — without the chaos of groups and without exposing anyone's phone number. Here's a no-nonsense checklist to get one working.

Set-up (15 minutes)

  • Use your business name exactly as customers know it
  • Add a clear profile photo or logo
  • Write a one-line description: what you post and how often
  • Copy the channel link and save it somewhere handy

Where to share the link

  • Instagram and YouTube bios
  • A QR code on bills, packaging and the shop counter
  • The end of every reel: "price list on our WhatsApp channel"
  • Your WhatsApp status once a week
  • Your Google Business Profile posts

What to post

  • New stock and restock alerts
  • Channel-only offers with a clear deadline
  • Quick polls — "which colour next?"
  • Short voice notes in Hindi or your regional language
  • Behind-the-scenes photos
Rule of thumb: 2–4 posts a week. More than that and people mute you. Muted followers are basically gone.
