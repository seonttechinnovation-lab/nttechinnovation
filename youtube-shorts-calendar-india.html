A 4-Week YouTube Shorts Calendar for Small Indian Channels

Most small channels don't run out of energy. They run out of ideas by day six. A calendar fixes that. Here's a four-week plan you can copy today.

Week 1: Quick tips

Post one 20-second tip a day from your niche. A Pune fitness coach can show one stretch. A Delhi food vlogger can show one street snack. Keep the hook in the first line.

Week 2: Before and after

People love a change they can see. A messy desk turned tidy. Raw dough turned into paratha. Show the result first and then the process.

Week 3: Answer comments

Pick one viewer question a day and reply with a Short. It costs nothing to plan and it tells viewers you read what they write.

Week 4: Mistakes and myths

Share one common mistake in your field each day. These get saved and shared a lot because people want to check if they do it too.

Three rules for every Short

  • Shoot vertical and keep it under 40 seconds.
  • Add on-screen text in Hindi or your regional language.
  • End with a reason to watch the next one.
