Free Instagram Views in India: A Plain Checklist That Works

Most "free views" tricks shared in Indian Telegram groups either don't work or risk your account. This checklist sticks to things Instagram itself rewards. No apps. No passwords. Just habits.

Before you post

  • Is it a Reel? Reels reach non-followers far more often than photos.
  • Does the first second show the result or the hook? If not, re-cut it.
  • Is the audio fresh and climbing, not one that peaked months ago?
  • Is there on-screen text for people watching with sound off?

When you post

  • Post during your audience's active hours on IST (check Insights).
  • Write a caption that asks one simple question.
  • Use 3 to 5 specific hashtags, not 30 random ones.

After you post

  • Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  • Share the Reel to your Story with a poll sticker.
  • After 48 hours, check 3-second hold and average watch time. Keep the hook style that won.

Skip these

  • Apps that ask for your Instagram password.
  • Engagement pods full of unrelated accounts.
  • Deleting and reposting the same Reel again and again.
