Starting an SMM Reseller Panel in Bangladesh: A Realistic First 90 Days

Plenty of students and freelancers in Bangladesh want to start their own SMM reseller panel. The pitch sounds easy: buy services wholesale, sell at a markup, keep the difference. It can work. But the first 90 days usually look different from the YouTube tutorials.

Days 1–30: Supplier and pricing

  • Pick one main supplier with an API and Bangladesh-relevant services.
  • Test 5–10 services yourself before listing them.
  • Price in Taka with a margin that covers refunds and drops, not just a flat double.

Days 31–60: First customers

  • Most early buyers are f-commerce page owners and small YouTubers you already know.
  • Reply fast. Support is what keeps resellers alive.
  • Log every order and its supplier order ID. You'll need it when someone asks "where's my order?"

Days 61–90: Keep or quit

  • Look at profit after refunds. Not revenue.
  • Drop services with high complaint rates.
  • Decide whether this is a side income or a real business.

Honest note: margins are thin and competition is high. The resellers who last usually win on support and trust, not on the lowest price.
