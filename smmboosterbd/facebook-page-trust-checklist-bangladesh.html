Facebook Page Trust Checklist for Bangladeshi Online Sellers

In Bangladesh, a buyer decides in seconds whether your Facebook page is real or a scam. Run through these 12 points once a month. Every "no" is a sale you might be losing.

Page basics

  1. Profile photo is your logo or real shop, not a stock image.
  2. Cover photo shows your actual products.
  3. About section has phone, area and delivery info (inside/outside Dhaka charges).
  4. Page name matches what customers search for.

Proof

  1. At least 10 real customer reviews or feedback screenshots.
  2. Recent posts within the last 3 days.
  3. Real product photos and short videos, not only supplier pictures.
  4. A visible follower base so new visitors don't land on an empty page.

Response

  1. Messenger replies within an hour during business hours.
  2. Prices visible on posts, so people don't have to inbox for everything.
  3. Clear payment options: cash on delivery, bKash.
  4. A pinned post explaining how to order and return policy.
