Ordering Instagram Services From a Panel in India: A Pre-Order Checklist

You found a panel. The prices look good. Before you paste your Instagram link and pay, run through this list. It takes five minutes and saves a lot of regret.

1. Is your account public?

Most services can't deliver to a private profile. Switch to public before ordering and keep it public until the order finishes.

2. Is the link correct?

Followers need the profile link. Likes and views need the post or Reel link. A wrong link is the most common reason an order fails.

3. Did you read the service description?

Look for start time, speed per day, drop rate and refill period. Two services with the same name can behave very differently.

4. Are you ordering a sensible amount?

A page with 300 followers jumping to 30,000 overnight looks odd to everyone. Small steps look natural.

5. Is your password safe?

No real panel needs it. If a site asks, leave.

6. Did you test with the minimum first?

One small order tells you more than ten reviews.

7. Do you have a content plan?

Numbers without good posts don't hold attention. Keep posting Reels through the week.
