Facebook Page Starter Checklist for Filipino Small Businesses

In the Philippines, Facebook is still where most buyers first check a small business. A milk tea stall in Cebu, a reseller in Davao, a home baker in Quezon City: customers look at the Page before they message. If the Page looks abandoned, they move on.

Before you think about growth, run through these ten items.

Profile basics

  • Use your real business name, the same one on your signage or Shopee store.
  • Upload a clear logo as the profile photo and a real product or shop photo as the cover.
  • Fill in the address, service area and opening hours, even if you only deliver.
  • Add a mobile number and a note on the e-wallets you accept, like GCash or Maya.

Trust signals

  • Post at least three times in the last two weeks so the Page looks alive.
  • Pin one post that shows your bestseller and price range.
  • Turn on Messenger instant replies in English and Tagalog.
  • Ask your first ten happy customers for a short recommendation.

Growth readiness

  • Check Meta Business Suite once a week for reach and message response time.
  • Only think about paid boosts or bought likes after the Page looks complete.

If you are weighing whether to buy Facebook likes in the Philippines, read the peso prices and the honest limits first. A complete Page converts those new visitors much better than an empty one.
