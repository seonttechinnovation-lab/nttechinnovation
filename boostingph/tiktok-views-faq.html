TikTok Views FAQ for Filipino Creators

Quick answers to the questions that come up in almost every Filipino creator group chat.

What counts as a view on TikTok?

A view counts as soon as the video starts playing, and replays count too. That is why views are always higher than the number of people who saw it.

Why are my views stuck at around 200?

  • The first test audience did not watch to the end.
  • The hook took too long, so people swiped in the first second.
  • The video looked reposted or had another app's watermark.

Does posting time matter in the Philippines?

It helps a little. Lunch break and evenings tend to be busy, but your own Analytics tab shows when your followers are actually online.

Should I delete videos that flop?

Usually no. Some videos pick up days later. Delete only if it breaks guidelines or hurts your brand.

Do paid views help?

  • They can make a new video look less empty to the first real viewers.
  • They will not fix a weak hook or low watch time.
  • Use them on your best video, in small amounts.
