A ₱1,000 Facebook Boost Planner for Filipino Small Shops

You have ₱1,000 and a Facebook page for your shop. Tap Boost, pick a random post and hope? Many owners do that. Here's a calmer way to spend it.

Before you spend: pick one goal

Messages or page likes. Not both. For most small shops in the Philippines, messages are the better goal because a chat can turn into an order today.

The one-week split

  • Days 1 to 2, ₱200. Boost two different posts at ₱100 each. This is your test.
  • Day 3, ₱0. Check which post brought more messages per peso.
  • Days 4 to 7, ₱800. Put the rest behind the winner at ₱200 a day.

Audience settings that matter

Choose your city or nearby barangays if you deliver locally. Set the age range to match real buyers. Skip broad nationwide targeting unless you ship everywhere.

The post itself

Use a real photo of the product. Put the price and how to order in the first two lines. Reply to every message within minutes while the boost runs, since slow replies waste the money.

After the week

Write down the total messages and total orders. Divide ₱1,000 by the orders. That's your cost per order, and it's the number to beat next month.
