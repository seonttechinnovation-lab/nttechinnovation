Which Social Media Platform Should a Filipino Business Start With?

Trying to be on every platform at once is how small teams burn out. Pick one, do it well, then expand. Here's a quick way to choose based on who your customers are and what you sell.

Do most of your buyers message you to order? Start with Facebook. Pages, Marketplace and Messenger still drive a big share of local selling in the Philippines.

Is your product visual and your audience under 30? Start with TikTok. Short demos, before-and-after clips and live selling work well.

Do you sell lifestyle, food, fashion or travel? Instagram is strong for aesthetics and creator collabs.

Can you teach or explain something? YouTube builds long-term search traffic and trust, though it's slower to start.

A simple rule

Go where your customers already scroll. Ask your last 20 buyers how they found you. Their answers beat any trend report.

After you choose

  • Post consistently for 30 days before judging results.
  • Reply to every comment and message fast.
  • Repurpose your best posts to a second platform later.
