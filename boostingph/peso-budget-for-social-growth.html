A Peso Budget Plan for Social Media Growth

Small businesses in the Philippines rarely have a marketing team. Usually it is the owner, a phone and whatever is left after rent and stock. That is fine. A simple split keeps spending sensible.

The 60-30-10 split

  • 60% on content. Better photos, a ring light, a simple editing app, product samples for videos.
  • 30% on targeted ads. Facebook or TikTok ads aimed at your city or delivery area.
  • 10% on boosting social proof. Only for your best posts, only after the page looks complete.

Putting it into practice

  • Even with only a few thousand pesos a month, most of it should still go into making better posts.
  • Run one small ad for a week, check messages and orders, then decide whether to repeat.
  • Use the boosting slice on one strong video or post, not spread thinly across ten.

Signs you are overspending

  • Likes go up but messages and orders do not.
  • You pay for reach on posts you would not share yourself.
  • You skip posting because "the ads will handle it".
