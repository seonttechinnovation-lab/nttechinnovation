Engagement Fixes for Filipino Pages: A 2-Week Experiment

Low engagement doesn't mean your audience doesn't care. Usually your posts just don't give them an easy reason to react. Run this two-week experiment and measure the difference yourself.

Week 1: Make replying easy

  • End every post with one simple question. "Alin mas gusto mo, A or B?" beats "What do you think?"
  • Use polls in Stories daily.
  • Reply to every comment within the first hour. Ask a follow-up question back.

Week 2: Make sharing worth it

  • Post one useful thing people want to save: a price list, a checklist, a "how to" carousel.
  • Post one relatable thing people want to tag friends in.
  • Go live once for 15 minutes and answer questions.

Track these three numbers

MetricWhy it matters
Comments per postShows people feel invited to talk
Shares and savesTells the algorithm your post is worth spreading
Reply speedFast replies keep threads alive

Compare these against the two weeks before. Keep what moved the numbers.
