YouTube Partner Program Progress Tracker for Filipino Channels

Most small channels in the Philippines check YouTube Studio every day and still have no idea if they are on track. A once-a-month tracker fixes that. Copy the fields below into a notebook, Google Sheet or even your phone notes.

What to record on the 1st of every month

  • Subscribers from the Studio dashboard.
  • Public watch hours (last 365 days) from the Earn tab.
  • Shorts views (last 90 days) if you are going the Shorts route.
  • Videos published that month, long-form and Shorts counted separately.
  • Top video by views and by new subscribers.

How to read it

  • Divide the subscribers you still need by your monthly gain. That is your rough months-to-go.
  • If watch hours grow but subscribers do not, add a clear subscribe reason in your videos.
  • If one topic wins two months in a row, make a series out of it.
  • Remember the 365-day window rolls, so old watch hours drop off.

Common Filipino-channel mistakes

  • Uploading reaction or compilation clips that later fail the reused-content review.
  • Switching between Tagalog, Bisaya and English audiences every week.
  • Stopping uploads during busy months, then restarting from zero momentum.
