TikTok Affiliate Readiness Check for Filipino Creators

A lot of Pinoy creators apply for TikTok Shop affiliate, get approved, add a yellow basket to their videos and then sell nothing. Usually the account just was not ready. Score yourself on the points below before you apply.

Account health

  • No recent community guideline strikes on the account.
  • Your account is set to the Philippines region.
  • Your profile photo, name and bio clearly say what you review or sell.

Content habits

  • You have posted at least three times a week for the last month.
  • Your videos show your face or your hands using a product, not just slideshows.
  • You already talk about products naturally, even without a commission.

Product fit

  • You know which category you will promote: beauty, gadgets, home, baby or food.
  • Your audience can afford the items, so check typical prices in pesos first.
  • You can get samples or already own the products you will feature.

Scoring

  • 8 or 9 points: apply now.
  • 5 to 7 points: fix the gaps for two weeks, then apply.
  • Under 5: focus on consistent posting first.
