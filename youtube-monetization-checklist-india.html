YouTube Monetization Checklist for Indian Channels

By MD Fozla Rabbi, SEO Specialist

Hitting the numbers is only half of YouTube monetization. Plenty of Indian channels reach the threshold and still get rejected — or wait months — because of things they never checked. Use this list before you click "Apply".

1. Eligibility

  • 1,000 subscribers
  • 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, or 10 million valid Shorts views in the last 90 days
  • No active Community Guidelines strikes
  • 2-Step Verification turned on for the Google account
  • Living in a country where YPP is available (India is)

Thresholds and timelines get updated now and then. For the current India-specific rules and any upcoming deadlines, check this up-to-date guide to YouTube monetization rules.

2. Content review

  • No reused content (compilations, re-uploads, TV clips) without real commentary
  • No copyrighted music without a license
  • Titles and thumbnails match what the video actually shows
  • Your recent videos represent the channel well — reviewers look at the latest uploads first

3. AdSense setup

  • One AdSense account per person — don't create a second one
  • Name matches your PAN and bank account
  • Postal address correct for the PIN verification letter
  • Tax info submitted in AdSense

4. Common reasons for rejection

  • "Reused content" — the most common one for Indian channels
  • Channels made mostly of short, low-effort clips
  • Sudden spikes in subscribers or views that don't match engagement
On paid growth: some creators try SMM panels such as SMMYodha to speed up the numbers. YouTube only counts valid watch time and reviews audience quality before approval, so artificial spikes can delay or block monetization. If you use any boost at all, keep it small and never rely on it for eligibility.

5. After approval

  • Turn on mid-roll ads for videos over 8 minutes
  • Check the Revenue tab weekly for RPM by video
  • Add memberships and Super Thanks once you have a loyal audience
